FAQ

Questions practices ask us

Does it submit anything to HMRC?
No. Intake Gate is a pre-submission quality gate. Nothing is filed with HMRC and there is no MTD submission — it tells you what to fix, then you file the way you already do.
How does it decide something is wrong?
Deterministic rules. Every flag carries a rule ID, a plain-English reason and the exact field it fired on. It's ordinary code, not an AI guess — so you can audit why anything failed.
Do I have to change my accounting software?
No. It sits in front of your existing workflow. Write-back connectors to accounting systems are on the roadmap; today it produces a gap report and a client chase message you can send in one click.
What can it catch that capture tools can't?
Bundle-level gaps: a missing month of bank statements, an entire account the client forgot, duplicates, and documents outside the declared period — all invisible to per-document capture.
How is it priced?
A monthly subscription scaled to how many active clients you run through it, with a free tier so you can trial it on a few clients first. Book a demo for plan details.
