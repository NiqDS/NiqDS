The problem

The most valuable hour in a practice is spent chasing clients.

Getting complete, correct records out of a client is the least-billable, most-hated task in bookkeeping — and today it's done by memory, spreadsheets, and back-and-forth email. Capture tools read documents; none of them tell you the bundle is incomplete or write the chase.

Today

A month of bank statements missing, a whole account forgotten, an invalid VAT number, a duplicate already paid — caught late, by hand, if at all. Each one is an awkward client email and lost time.

With Intake Gate

The bundle is checked in seconds against 20+ UK rules. You get a gap report and a ready-to-send chase naming exactly what to fix — every flag traceable to a named rule.

Why now

A regulatory deadline just made a chronic pain acute — and quarterly.

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax turns record-keeping from one annual return into four quarterly submissions, on a falling threshold. The chase quadruples, and a wave of first-time digital filers enters the system with messy records.

The insight

Auditable AI — the part that makes it sellable to a liable buyer.

Our architecture splits the job in two: an AI model extracts each document into structured data, then deterministic code makes every pass/fail decision. No accountant has to trust a black box — they can point at a named rule and see exactly why something failed.

Why it's defensible The moat isn't the AI — everyone has AI. It's the explainable, testable UK rule library a professionally-liable buyer can rely on, plus ownership of the chase workflow. It's the one design a black-box competitor structurally can't copy without rebuilding trust from zero.

The 60-second demo: a broken bundle goes in → the screen shows red → a plain-English fix list → a chase email you can send. That product exists today.

Non-dilutive funding

A genuine innovation story for grants — told honestly.

The fundable innovation is not “an AI product” — it's explainable AI for regulated, professionally-liable finance: a hybrid where AI extraction is bounded by deterministic, auditable rules so its decisions can be trusted and tested. That's a real R&D question (trustworthy/verifiable AI) and it maps cleanly to UK innovation funding.

Avenues to explore Innovate UK Smart Grants / sector competitions · R&D tax relief on the engineering already done · AI-adoption and productivity programmes. Non-dilutive funding can extend runway alongside a pre-seed round — not replace the commercial proof from Phase 0.

Positioning note: we lead with “auditable AI”, never “fully autonomous AI”. Overclaiming autonomy would both fail diligence and undercut the exact trust that makes the product sellable.
