Intake Gate · strategy

Business Model, PESTLE & SWOT

Intake Gate is the records-QA layer for UK bookkeeping practices: it catches incomplete or wrong client document bundles on arrival and drafts the exact chase email. Below: the Osterwalder Business Model Canvas plus a PESTLE and SWOT analysis.

Osterwalder · Business Model Canvas

The nine building blocks

At the centre sits the value proposition: an auditable check (the model extracts, deterministic code decides) you can sell to a professionally-liable buyer.

8

Key partners

  • Accounting software: Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent (directories, app marketplaces)
  • Professional bodies: AAT, ICB, IAB, ICAEW
  • Capture tools (Dext, AutoEntry) — complement/import, not competitors
  • LLM providers (Anthropic / OpenAI) — extraction
  • Google / Microsoft — OAuth for email drafts; hosting
7

Key activities

  • Building & maintaining the UK rule library (hold zero false BLOCKs)
  • Document-extraction accuracy
  • Sales/marketing around MTD deadlines; practice onboarding
  • Integrations (write-back to accounting systems)
6

Key resources

  • Deterministic rule library + eval harness — the core auditable asset
  • Codebase: engine (FastAPI), iOS app, JSON API, OAuth
  • Domain expertise (UK bookkeeping, MTD)
  • Design partners, real data, case studies & testimonials
1

Value propositions

  • For practices: save the most expensive unbillable hour — chasing documents; a ready-to-send chase email
  • Auditable: every flag = a named rule with a reason, not a black box
  • Bundle-level checks: missing month of statements, a whole forgotten account, duplicates, out-of-period docs — what capture tools don't do
  • For SMEs: a "green light" before sending to the accountant; less back-and-forth
  • Nothing filed with HMRC — pre-submission QA; exact arithmetic (Decimal), UK rules (VAT check digit)
4

Customer relationships

  • Phase 0 — concierge / high-touch (design partners)
  • Then — self-serve SaaS + onboarding + support
  • Referral loop: SME ⇄ accountant
  • Trust via audit trail and client-safe copy
3

Channels

  • Direct/founder-led sales; professional bodies & their events
  • AccountingWEB, LinkedIn, bookkeeper Facebook groups
  • Xero/QBO/FreeAgent advisor directories + marketplaces
  • MTD-deadline content; web & iOS app
2

Customer segments

  • Core: small UK practices & independent bookkeepers (5–40 clients), especially with MTD clients
  • Wedge/funnel: micro-businesses — sole traders & landlords under MTD
  • Later: larger firms / franchises — white-label
9

Cost structure

  • Engineering & rule maintenance — the main line
  • LLM API — variable extraction cost (mock/deterministic part is cheap)
  • Hosting/infrastructure (Postgres, file storage)
  • Sales/marketing (CAC); legal/compliance (GDPR, ICO, OAuth verification); support
  • Value >> price (≈10×) — the lever is distribution, not justifying the cost
5

Revenue streams

  • SaaS subscription; value metric — active clients per month
  • Tiers: Free / Starter ~£39–49 / Practice ~£129–149 / Scale (custom); SME Solo ~£6–9 or free via the accountant
  • Overage for extra clients; annual billing at a discount (~2 months free)
  • Later — premium modules (onboarding/AML, CIS, landlord), white-label, integrations
  • Non-dilutive: Innovate UK grants, R&D tax relief
Macro-environment · PESTLE

Factors — and what each means for us

Headline: MTD (the shift to quarterly digital reporting) is the strongest tailwind, and an auditable, deterministic architecture is the answer to low trust in "AI black boxes".

P

Political

  • MTD is an HMRC government initiative — a strong political demand driver
  • Deadlines have slipped before — risk of further delay
  • Drive to digitise tax and small business; rising AI regulation
What it meansDon't bet the company on one date: the "incomplete records" pain exists without MTD — MTD just makes it quarterly and urgent.
E

Economic

  • Fragmented market: ~40k accountancy firms, ~17k bookkeeping firms
  • Willingness to pay proven (Dext ~£190–240/mo for 10 clients)
  • A bookkeeper-hour ~£30–40 — time saved easily justifies the price
  • Recession pressures spend, but compliance is non-optional
What it meansValue per client (~£120–160/yr) is an order of magnitude above price — focus on distribution.
S

Social

  • ~864k new digital filers, many unprepared
  • Professionally-liable accountants distrust "black box" tools
  • Small practices often not tech-first; SMEs expect mobile-first
What it meansSell auditability and simplicity ("green light", plain English), not "AI".
T

Technological

  • LLMs improving and getting cheaper (better extraction)
  • Mature integration APIs (Xero/QBO/FreeAgent)
  • LLM-provider dependency risk; risk that Dext adds validation
What it meansThe deterministic layer lowers model risk and builds a moat; the adapter keeps the provider swappable.
L

Legal

  • UK GDPR / DPA: financial-data processing, processor role, DPAs with clients, ICO registration
  • AML: an onboarding module checks pack completeness only, not risk judgement
  • Regulated-services line: we are QA, not tax advice; nothing filed with HMRC
  • OAuth verification (Google/Microsoft); LLM data terms
What it means"Records QA, not advice" + least privilege (drafts only) keeps us out of the regulated zone.
E

Environmental

  • Direct impact minimal (SaaS); digitising reduces paper
  • LLM/data-centre carbon footprint — an ESG factor in provider/hosting choice
  • Client ESG reporting may become a driver later
What it meansLeast relevant factor now; keep as an ESG line for future tenders.
Internal × external · SWOT

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats

Strategy link: press strength × opportunity (auditability × the MTD wave) and close the weaknesses (prove accuracy on real data, add an accounting advisor) before scaling.

S

Strengths

Internal +
  • Deterministic, auditable rules engine — sellable to a liable buyer; a real moat
  • Bundle-level checks competitors don't do (missing statement, forgotten account)
  • Working prototype: full loop, 81 tests, zero false BLOCKs, web + iOS + JSON API + OAuth drafts
  • UK-specific rules (VAT check digit, MTD period logic)
  • Provider-agnostic LLM adapter — low model lock-in
W

Weaknesses

Internal −
  • Pre-revenue: no customers or traction, pricing unproven
  • Small team, no named accounting advisor yet (credibility gap)
  • "Feature not a company" risk — narrow initial scope
  • Extraction accuracy on messy scans unproven at scale
  • Prototype-grade auth/security; no multi-tenancy or deployment (Phase 1 work)
O

Opportunities

External +
  • MTD ITSA (2026→2028): ~864k, then millions, into quarterly filing
  • Fragmented market (~40k firms) with proven willingness to pay
  • Adjacent modules (onboarding/AML, CIS, landlord, VAT-period) raise ACV
  • SME wedge → referral loop into practices; non-dilutive grants
  • Xero/QBO marketplace listings; white-label for larger firms
T

Threats

External −
  • Incumbents (Dext/AutoEntry) could add validation
  • MTD deadlines could slip again (timing risk)
  • LLM-provider dependency (price/quality/policy)
  • Drift into "tax advice"/AML judgement if the line isn't held
  • GDPR/data-security incidents; long, conservative sales cycles
Internal working draft. Market figures (firm counts, MTD audience, competitor prices) are from public sources and shown as indicative; tariffs are hypotheses to validate in Phase 0. Intake Gate performs records quality assurance, gives no tax advice, and files nothing with HMRC.
