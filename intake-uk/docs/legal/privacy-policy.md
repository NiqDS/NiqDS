## 1. Who we are and our two roles
Intake Gate provides document-intake quality-assurance software to UK bookkeeping
practices.
- For **your account and enquiry data**, we are the **controller**.
- For the **client documents a practice processes through the service**, the
  practice is the **controller** and we act as a **processor** on its instructions
  (governed by our Data Processing Agreement).

## 2. What we collect
- **Account & enquiry data** — name, work email, practice name, messages you send us.
- **Document data** — files you upload and the fields extracted from them, processed

- **Usage & technical data** — log data, IP address, and basic analytics `[only if
  you enable an analytics tool — see Cookies]`.
